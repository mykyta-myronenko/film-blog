Hello, I'm Mykyta (or Nick), a Ukrainian artist based in UK, London.

I have a tendency to wear "too many hats"^[architecture, animation, film, programming, 3d-modelling, music]. "And that's not a bad thing. It's actually a strength!" is what I tell myself everyday. Can't say it's not fun to try new things.

At the moment I'm fixated on film making, and this is a place where I will procrastinate by writing about the process. 

# Reach Out!

If you want to make something together, or are a fond of building a local community of artists feel free to reach out. <3
